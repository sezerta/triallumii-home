3. Data We Collect

The App does not require user registration, does not ask you to create an account, and does not directly collect your name, phone number, photo, or precise location.

The following data is collected automatically or by our third-party partners (Google AdMob for advertising; Apple and RevenueCat for in-app purchases):

3.1 Device and Technical Information

  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro)
  • iOS version
  • Device language and region
  • Anonymous device identifier (IDFV)

3.2 Advertising Identifiers

  • IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers): collected only if you tap "Allow" on the Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline, IDFA is not collected; ads continue to display but are non-personalized.
  • IP address (used for coarse geographic inference, not precise location)

3.3 Ad Interaction Data

  • Ad impression count, click count, completed rewarded video count (collected by Google AdMob)

3.4 In-Game Event Data (Local)

  • Current level, stars earned, unlocked skins/cosmetics, high score — these are stored only on your device and synced via Apple iCloud Key-Value Store; they are not sent to our servers.

3.5 In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases: consumable coin packs ($0.99 / $1.99 / $3.99) and a one-time non-consumable "Remove Ads" purchase ($4.99). All purchases are processed entirely by Apple (StoreKit); the App never collects or sees your credit-card or payment data.

  • Purchase receipts and an anonymous app user ID are validated via RevenueCat for entitlement management (i.e., to determine which purchases you own). See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Purchases are non-refundable through the App; refund requests are handled by Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com.

3.6 Game Center

  • Apple Game Center is used for optional leaderboards; scores and Game Center player identifiers are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

3.7 Data We Do NOT Collect

The following data is never collected:

  • Name, date of birth, email address, phone number
  • Precise location (GPS)
  • Photos, camera, microphone, health, calendar, contacts
  • Credit-card or payment data (all in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details)

6. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS 14.5 and above, when you first launch the App, you will see Apple's mandatory tracking prompt:

"Jet Fighter would like permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites."
  • If you allow: AdMob will use the IDFA to deliver more relevant ads. Our ad revenue is higher, helping sustain the project.
  • If you deny: IDFA is not collected. Ads will still appear, but they will be random / contextual rather than personalized. Your gameplay experience is unchanged.

You can change this choice at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Jet Fighter.

7. GDPR — Rights of EU/UK Users

If you are a resident of the European Union, the European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right of access — to know what data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — to request deletion.
  • Right to restrict processing.
  • Right to data portability.
  • Right to object — to legitimate-interest-based processing.
  • Right not to be subject to automated decision-making.

On first launch, if your IP indicates an EU/UK location, Google AdMob's Consent Management Platform (CMP) prompt will appear, asking for ad-cookie consent. If you refuse, ads will be served as contextual (non-personalized).

The data controller has not appointed an EU representative under GDPR Art. 27, as the App does not regularly target EU residents in a manner triggering that obligation. EU users may contact sezerta@gmail.com directly for any request.

8. Children (Under 13 / COPPA)

  • The App's App Store age rating is 9+.
  • The App does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from this age group.
  • The "Tag for child-directed treatment (COPPA)" flag is not enabled in our AdMob configuration; however, if ATT is denied, IDFA is not collected, providing an additional protective layer for younger users.
  • If a parent discovers that their child's personal data has been collected, please contact sezerta@gmail.com — the data will be deleted within 30 days.
