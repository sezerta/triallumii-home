ORBYT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game ORBYT ("the App", "we") handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

No account, no personal data we collect directly

ORBYT does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. Your game progress (best scores, level, unlocked skins, daily streak, settings) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of ORBYT shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain information, such as a device advertising identifier (IDFA), approximate usage data, and device information. This data is used to deliver and measure ads. On iOS, we ask for your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before any tracking, and we present a consent choice (Google UMP) where required by law (e.g. EEA/UK). You can decline.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can remove all advertising by purchasing the Remove Ads in-app purchase.

In-app purchases

Purchases (e.g. "Remove Ads") are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple's handling of your data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

ORBYT is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are configured to request non-personalized/appropriate content where required.

Your choices

  • Decline tracking in the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Adjust your consent choices (where shown).
  • Buy "Remove Ads" to stop all advertising.
  • Delete the App to remove all locally stored game data.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
